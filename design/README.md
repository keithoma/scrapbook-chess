# Game design

These documents serve the game designer and implementers. They describe intended
experience, agreed rules, unfinished mechanics, and authored ideas. They do not
assert that every feature is implemented. Current technical behavior belongs in
[docs/](../docs/README.md), and executable achievement content lives in
[data/achievements/](../data/achievements/).

## Product

[Product experience](product/experience.md) defines the audience, reward loop,
platform collection, eligibility, and replay experience.

## Mechanics

- [Achievement families](mechanics/achievement-families.md) describes badges,
  mastery, story, and scraps.
- [Opening masteries](mechanics/opening-masteries.md) defines named openings,
  independent colors, theory participation, and conversion rewards.
- [Checkmate scraps](mechanics/checkmate-scraps.md) defines real and virtual
  collection behavior and the remaining design choices.

## Content

- [Implemented Caro-Kann masteries](content/caro-kann-masteries.md) lists every
  current identity and its grouped aliases for reviewing catalog granularity.
- [Achievement ideas](content/achievement-ideas.md) preserves draft mechanics,
  episode ideas, and shelved concepts.
- [Quote ideas](content/quote-ideas.md) preserves writing references.

## Planning

[Achievement implementation map](planning/achievement-implementation.md) connects
the catalog to required detectors, open decisions, and the requested milestones.

These are subject-based design sections, not Diátaxis categories. Keep agreed
rules in product/mechanics, catalog reviews and draft writing in content, and
implementation planning in planning. Link between them rather than repeating the same rule. Mark a
proposal or open question explicitly. See the [documentation guide](../docs/how-to/write-documentation.md)
for maintenance and file-size rules.
